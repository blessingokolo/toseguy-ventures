WHO WE ARE

Toseguy Ventures began its journey 11 years ago as a small retail business with a big vision: to make quality printing materials, advertising supplies and essential merchandise accessible to businesses and individuals.

What started as a modest retail operation has grown into a wholesale supply and importation business, driven by determination, customer trust and a strong commitment to quality.

Over the years, we have expanded our operations to include sourcing and importing quality materials from China, enabling us to serve the growing needs of Nigeria's printing and advertising industries.

Today, Toseguy Ventures specialises in flex banners for printing, PVC tarpaulins, carpentry accessories and general merchandise. We serve retailers, wholesalers, printing companies, advertising agencies and individuals seeking reliable products at competitive prices.

From humble beginnings in retail to a growing wholesale and importation business, our journey reflects 11 years of growth, resilience and commitment to customer satisfaction.

At Toseguy Ventures, our promise remains simple: delivering quality and value every step of the way. Thank you for being part of our story.
